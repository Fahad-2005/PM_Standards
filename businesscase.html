PMBOK 7 Approach

Core Philosophy

  • Value delivery central theme
  • Benefits realization focus
  • Outcome-oriented measures

Unique Elements

  • Value-driven performance domains
  • Continuous benefits assessment
  • Strategic alignment emphasis
📗

PRINCE2 Approach

Core Philosophy

  • Business case as central theme
  • Continuous justification requirement
  • Benefits review at stage gates

Unique Elements

  • Mandatory business case document
  • Investment appraisal methods
  • Benefits realization management
📙

ISO 21500 Approach

Core Philosophy

  • Organizational value alignment
  • Strategic objective linkage
  • Portfolio-level benefits view

Unique Elements

  • Enterprise-wide benefits tracking
  • Organizational strategy integration
  • Governance-driven justification

🔍 Comparative Analysis

🤝 Similarities

  • All require clear business justification
  • Benefits identification and tracking essential
  • Value delivery measurement important
  • Continuous viability assessment needed

⚡ Key Differences

  • Emphasis: PMBOK (value delivery), PRINCE2 (business case), ISO (strategic alignment)
  • Approach: PMBOK (principle-based), PRINCE2 (document-centric), ISO (enterprise-level)
  • Monitoring: PMBOK (continuous), PRINCE2 (stage-gate), ISO (portfolio view)

🎯 Best Use Cases

  • PMBOK: Value-driven, outcome-focused projects
  • PRINCE2: Investment-heavy projects requiring formal justification
  • ISO: Enterprise portfolios with strategic alignment needs
