PMBOK 7 Approach

Core Philosophy

  • Governance across performance domains
  • Decision rights and oversight activities
  • Strategic alignment emphasis

Unique Elements

  • Cross-cutting governance concern
  • Principle-based governance approach
  • Adaptive governance structures
📗

PRINCE2 Approach

Core Philosophy

  • Prescriptive governance structure
  • Clear authority levels and tolerances
  • Exception-based escalation

Unique Elements

  • Mandatory governance structure
  • Tolerance-based authority levels
  • Exception reporting mechanisms
📙

ISO 21500 Approach

Core Philosophy

  • Organizational policy integration
  • Management system alignment
  • Compliance and control emphasis

Unique Elements

  • Organizational system integration
  • High-level governance guidance
  • Compliance framework alignment

🔍 Comparative Analysis

🤝 Similarities

  • Clear accountability and decision-making required
  • Governance links projects to strategic expectations
  • Authority levels and escalation mechanisms
  • Regulatory and compliance considerations

⚡ Key Differences

  • Structure: PMBOK (flexible), PRINCE2 (prescriptive), ISO (organizational)
  • Focus: PMBOK (domains), PRINCE2 (roles), ISO (policy)
  • Approach: PMBOK (adaptive), PRINCE2 (formal), ISO (systematic)

🎯 Best Use Cases

  • PMBOK: Flexible governance environments
  • PRINCE2: Formal organizational structures
  • ISO: Compliance-driven organizations
