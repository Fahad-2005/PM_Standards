PMBOK 7 Approach

Core Philosophy

  • Quality embedded in delivery domain
  • Plan, assure, and control quality
  • Continuous improvement focus

Unique Elements

  • Cost of quality consideration
  • Quality metrics and measures
  • Value-driven quality decisions
📗

PRINCE2 Approach

Core Philosophy

  • Quality as central theme
  • Product-based quality criteria
  • Quality review technique

Unique Elements

  • Quality register maintenance
  • Product Description quality criteria
  • Customer quality expectations
📙

ISO 21500 Approach

Core Philosophy

  • Quality as subject group
  • ISO 9001 alignment potential
  • Organizational quality standards

Unique Elements

  • Plan and perform quality assurance
  • Perform quality control processes
  • Management system integration

🔍 Comparative Analysis

🤝 Similarities

  • All emphasize quality planning upfront
  • Quality assurance and control processes
  • Customer satisfaction orientation
  • Continuous improvement principles

⚡ Key Differences

  • Integration: PMBOK (domain-based), PRINCE2 (theme-based), ISO (process group)
  • Focus: PMBOK (process quality), PRINCE2 (product quality), ISO (system quality)
  • Standards: PMBOK (flexible), PRINCE2 (defined criteria), ISO (ISO 9001 alignment)

🎯 Best Use Cases

  • PMBOK: Projects requiring comprehensive quality management
  • PRINCE2: Product-focused projects with clear quality criteria
  • ISO: Organizations with ISO 9001 or quality management systems
