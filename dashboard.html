🤝 Similarities Summary

  • Risk Management: All three emphasize continuous risk assessment and mitigation strategies
  • Stakeholder Engagement: Common focus on identification and communication
  • Quality Management: Shared principles of quality planning and control
  • Project Closure: Universal emphasis on lessons learned and knowledge transfer

⚡ Key Differences

  • PMBOK 7: Principle-based, performance domains approach with flexible tailoring
  • PRINCE2: Product-focused, stage-gate methodology with defined roles
  • ISO 21500: High-level guidelines, organizational context and governance focus
  • Terminology: Different names for similar concepts across standards

🎯 Unique Elements

  • PMBOK Only: Performance domains, principle-based tailoring framework
  • PRINCE2 Only: Business case theme, product-based planning approach
  • ISO Only: Portfolio/programme integration, governance and compliance focus
