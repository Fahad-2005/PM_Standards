PMBOK 7 Approach

Core Philosophy

  • Schedule management within planning domain
  • Activity sequencing and dependencies
  • Critical path and schedule optimization

Unique Elements

  • Network diagramming techniques
  • Schedule compression methods
  • Iterative and rolling wave scheduling
📗

PRINCE2 Approach

Core Philosophy

  • Stage-based timeline management
  • Product delivery scheduling
  • Time tolerances and controls

Unique Elements

  • Checkpoint reports for timing
  • Stage boundary time reviews
  • Product-driven scheduling
📙

ISO 21500 Approach

Core Philosophy

  • Time as subject group process
  • Schedule development and control
  • Organizational timing standards

Unique Elements

  • Define activities and sequences
  • Estimate and develop schedule
  • Portfolio-level timing alignment

🔍 Comparative Analysis

🤝 Similarities

  • All require timeline development and control
  • Activity sequencing and dependencies recognized
  • Duration estimation essential
  • Schedule baseline or equivalent needed

⚡ Key Differences

  • Techniques: PMBOK (CPM, PERT), PRINCE2 (stage-based), ISO (process-driven)
  • Granularity: PMBOK (detailed), PRINCE2 (stage-focused), ISO (high-level)
  • Flexibility: PMBOK (adaptive), PRINCE2 (controlled), ISO (organizational)

🎯 Best Use Cases

  • PMBOK: Complex projects with intricate dependencies
  • PRINCE2: Stage-driven projects with clear milestones
  • ISO: Organizations with standardized scheduling processes
