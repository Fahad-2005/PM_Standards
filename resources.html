PMBOK 7 Approach

Core Philosophy

  • Resource and cost within planning domain
  • Earned Value Management (EVM)
  • Cost baseline and budget control

Unique Elements

  • Bottom-up and parametric estimation
  • Resource leveling and smoothing
  • Three-point cost estimation
📗

PRINCE2 Approach

Core Philosophy

  • Cost as part of business case theme
  • Stage-based budget allocation
  • Cost tolerance management

Unique Elements

  • Stage budget authorization
  • Exception-based cost reporting
  • Benefits vs. cost tracking
📙

ISO 21500 Approach

Core Philosophy

  • Resource and cost subject groups
  • Organizational resource standards
  • Budget development and control

Unique Elements

  • Estimate and control costs processes
  • Establish project team process
  • Portfolio-level resource optimization

🔍 Comparative Analysis

🤝 Similarities

  • All require resource planning and allocation
  • Cost estimation and budgeting essential
  • Financial tracking and control mechanisms
  • Resource optimization emphasized

⚡ Key Differences

  • Techniques: PMBOK (EVM, reserves), PRINCE2 (tolerances), ISO (organizational standards)
  • Control: PMBOK (detailed tracking), PRINCE2 (exception-based), ISO (process-driven)
  • Focus: PMBOK (performance measurement), PRINCE2 (business justification), ISO (systematic)

🎯 Best Use Cases

  • PMBOK: Complex projects requiring detailed cost control
  • PRINCE2: Business case-driven projects with stage budgets
  • ISO: Organizations with standardized resource processes
