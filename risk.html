Unique Elements

🔍 Comparative Analysis

🤝 Similarities

  • All emphasize continuous risk monitoring
  • Common risk identification techniques
  • Stakeholder communication importance
  • Risk response planning required

⚡ Key Differences

  • Integration: PMBOK (domains), PRINCE2 (themes), ISO (governance)
  • Focus: PMBOK (value), PRINCE2 (products), ISO (organization)
  • Approach: PMBOK (adaptive), PRINCE2 (staged), ISO (systematic)

🎯 Best Use Cases

  • PMBOK: Complex, adaptive projects
  • PRINCE2: Controlled, product-driven projects
  • ISO: Organizational standardization
