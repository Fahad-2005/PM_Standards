PMBOK 7 Approach

Core Philosophy

  • Integrated across all performance domains
  • Principle-based risk thinking
  • Continuous risk monitoring

Unique Elements

  • Performance domain integration
  • Principle-based tailoring
  • Value-driven risk decisions

PRINCE2 Approach

Core Philosophy

  • Risk theme throughout project
  • Product-focused risk assessment
  • Stage-gate risk reviews

Unique Elements

  • Risk theme integration
  • Product-based risk analysis
  • Stage gate risk decisions
📙

ISO 21500 Approach

Core Philosophy

  • Organizational risk context
  • High-level risk guidance
  • Stakeholder risk awareness

Unique Elements

  • Organizational context emphasis
  • Portfolio/programme alignment
  • Governance integration

🔍 Comparative Analysis

🤝 Similarities

  • All emphasize continuous risk monitoring
  • Common risk identification techniques
  • Stakeholder communication importance
  • Risk response planning required

⚡ Key Differences

  • Integration: PMBOK (domains), PRINCE2 (themes), ISO (governance)
  • Focus: PMBOK (value), PRINCE2 (products), ISO (organization)
  • Approach: PMBOK (adaptive), PRINCE2 (staged), ISO (systematic)

🎯 Best Use Cases

  • PMBOK: Complex, adaptive projects
  • PRINCE2: Controlled, product-driven projects
  • ISO: Organizational standardization
