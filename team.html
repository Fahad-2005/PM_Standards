PMBOK 7 Approach

Core Philosophy

  • Team performance domain emphasis
  • Servant leadership approach
  • Self-organizing teams support

Unique Elements

  • Team development stages (Tuckman)
  • Emotional intelligence focus
  • Distributed and virtual teams
📗

PRINCE2 Approach

Core Philosophy

  • Organization theme - defined roles
  • Clear role responsibilities
  • Three levels of management

Unique Elements

  • Project Board, Manager, Team Manager
  • Accountability and authority clarity
  • Role description documentation
📙

ISO 21500 Approach

Core Philosophy

  • Establish project team process
  • Develop project team
  • Organizational resource policies

Unique Elements

  • Resource subject group processes
  • Organizational culture alignment
  • Competency and training focus

🔍 Comparative Analysis

🤝 Similarities

  • All emphasize clear roles and responsibilities
  • Team development and competency building
  • Leadership and motivation importance
  • Collaborative culture promotion

⚡ Key Differences

  • Structure: PMBOK (flexible), PRINCE2 (prescriptive roles), ISO (organizational)
  • Leadership: PMBOK (servant leadership), PRINCE2 (hierarchical), ISO (competency-based)
  • Focus: PMBOK (performance), PRINCE2 (accountability), ISO (standards)

🎯 Best Use Cases

  • PMBOK: Adaptive teams, agile environments
  • PRINCE2: Formal organizations with clear hierarchies
  • ISO: Large enterprises with standardized HR processes
